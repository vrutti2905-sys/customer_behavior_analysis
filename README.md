# customer_behavior_analysis
Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis using Python, SQL, and Power BI. Analyzes 3,900 purchase records to identify customer trends, spending patterns, product preferences, and subscription behavior, helping businesses improve customer loyalty and sales.

Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis
Project Overview
This project analyzes 3,900 customer purchase records to understand
shopping patterns, spending habits, product preferences, discount usage,
and subscription behavior.
Objectives
- Understand customer spending patterns and product preferences.
- Compare revenue across customer groups.
- Analyze discounts, shipping types, and subscription behavior.
- Segment customers based on purchase history.
- Present insights to support business decisions.
Dataset
- Records: 3,900
- Columns: 18
- Missing values: 37 values in the Review Rating column
- Key fields: Customer demographics, purchase details, product
  categories, purchase amounts, discounts, review ratings, shipping
  types, and subscription status.
Tools and Technologies
- Python
- pandas
- SQL
- Microsoft Power BI
Project Workflow
1. Data Cleaning: Used Python and pandas to inspect and clean the
   dataset.
2. Missing-Value Handling: Filled missing review ratings using the
   median rating for each product category.
3. Feature Engineering: Created age groups and purchase-frequency
   features.
4. SQL Analysis: Investigated revenue by gender, discount usage,
   product ratings, shipping types, subscriptions, customer segments,
   and revenue by age group.
5. Power BI Dashboard: Visualized shopping patterns and business
   insights.
Business Questions
- How does revenue differ by gender?
- Which discount users spend above the average purchase amount?
- Which products have the highest average review ratings?
- How do average purchase amounts compare across shipping types?
- How do subscribers and non-subscribers differ in spending and
  revenue?
- Which products rely most heavily on discounts?
- How can customers be segmented into new, returning, and loyal
  groups?
- Which products are most purchased within each category?
- Are repeat buyers more likely to subscribe?
- Which age groups contribute the most revenue?
Business Recommendations
- Promote useful subscription benefits.
- Reward repeat customers through loyalty programs.
- Review discount policies to balance sales and profitability.
- Highlight highly rated and best-selling products.
- Use customer and age-group insights for targeted marketing.
